{% wireitem "01", "Warsaw — 17 Jul", "Poland moves on next K2 tranche as local assembly line takes shape", "Land · Poland · South Korea" %}
Negotiations on the follow-on batch of K2 main battle tanks advanced this week, with the localised K2PL configuration and technology-transfer terms reported as the remaining sticking points. Warsaw's armour recapitalisation — Korean K2s and K9 guns alongside US Abrams — remains Europe's largest by volume.

The strategic subtext is industrial: each tranche shifts more work into Polish plants, part of a deliberate bet that assembly lines are as much a deterrent as the tanks themselves.
{% endwireitem %}

{% wireitem "02", "Berlin — 16 Jul", "Berlin adds IRIS-T SLM batteries as the export queue lengthens", "Air Defence · Germany" %}
Germany committed to additional IRIS-T SLM air-defence batteries for the Bundeswehr, joining an order book already stretched by European Sky Shield partners and battlefield-proven demand from Ukraine. Diehl's production ramp — not the system's performance — is now the programme's limiting factor.
{% endwireitem %}

{% wireitem "03", "Washington — 15 Jul", "Army locks in multiyear interceptor buys to keep the PAC-3 line pouring", "Air Defence · United States" %}
Contract modifications announced this week extend multiyear procurement of PAC-3 MSE interceptors, the munition at the centre of every recent air-defence engagement from Kyiv to the Gulf. Multiyear authority — long resisted as budgetary handcuffs — is now the Pentagon's preferred tool for convincing industry to add capacity.
{% endwireitem %}

{% wireitem "04", "Tehran — 15 Jul", "State media unveils \"new\" Shahed variant; analysts see incremental changes", "Middle East · UAS" %}
Iranian outlets showcased what was described as an upgraded one-way attack drone with improved range and a jam-resistant seeker. Independent analysts assess the airframe as an incremental Shahed-136 derivative; as ever with Tehran's rollouts, the message is the production rate, not the prototype.
{% endwireitem %}

{% wireitem "05", "London — 14 Jul", "GCAP demonstrator work advances as partners talk export rules", "Air · UK · Italy · Japan" %}
Assembly of the UK's supersonic demonstrator for the Global Combat Air Programme continued on schedule, while London, Rome and Tokyo worked through the export-control framework that will decide whether the sixth-generation fighter can be sold beyond the trio. The 2035 in-service date still holds — on paper.
{% endwireitem %}
